Sampling rule (per Audit Programme V1.0): random_n = 25 orders selected via stratified-random across the period (8 weekly buckets, ≈3 per bucket). The 100% strata are the union of all orders where prescriber_action ∈ ('rejected','intervention') OR order.has_switch = true (different product or dose vs the patient's previous order). Strata may overlap — rows are de-duplicated by order_id with the most-stringent stratum label retained. Re-generating the sample creates a new seed and replaces all unreviewed rows; reviewed rows are preserved across re-generations.
