Decoupled from internal SLA The patient-facing SLA copy is a soft commitment shown in the app at order placement and on the Clinical Check status screen. It is intentionally separate from the internal warm/breach thresholds above — set to a number that's realistic for what patients should expect, not your internal ops target.
