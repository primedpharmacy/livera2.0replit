🚦 Reorder behaviour toggles
Per-clinic feature gates
Block reorder if monthly check-in overdue
Patient cannot reorder until they complete the monthly check-in · per BLD-15.6 · drives "Check-in required" state on patient app
Auto-prefill reorder questionnaire
Pre-populate reorder questionnaire with values from previous order · patient confirms or amends · default ON
Require BMI on every reorder
If OFF, BMI captured at registration is reused unless >180d old (Group 6 B2 trigger). If ON, every reorder prompts fresh BMI · slower but stronger evidence trail
