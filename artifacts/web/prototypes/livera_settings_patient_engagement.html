Patient engagement · BLD-DAY19-01 (DEC-30) · clinic-configurable patient nudges across the treatment lifecycle. Each nudge is independently togglable, per-clinic configurable, and audit-logged. Stored as clinic_config.day_X_nudge object array — one entry per active nudge. Patient app reads on session start; updates take effect on next nudge fire window. Visible to Owner and RM · all other roles see read-only · Coach role sees coaching-related nudges only. Nudge dispatch goes via Postmark (email), FCM (push), and Omnisend (SMS, Pass 2). Suppression rules and conversion stats refresh hourly.
