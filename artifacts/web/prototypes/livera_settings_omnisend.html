Omnisend integration · BLD-INT-OMNI-01 · marketing automation platform for email and SMS campaigns. Data flows are unidirectional Livera → Omnisend except for unsubscribe / bounce / engagement webhooks (Omnisend → Livera). Audience segments auto-rebuild every 15 minutes via cron sync.
